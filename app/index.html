    Road to the Platinum

    The order to earn them in, built from the official descriptions: story first, the one missable trophy early, then the character strands, collectibles, activities, and the crime and money trophies — with 100% completion last. There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies, so the platinum is long, not hard. Cheats are reported to disable trophies, so leave them off for the whole run.

    One missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. Lucia’s balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 counts toward the 18 and disappears after that chapter, and the game has no mission replay. Do it in Chapter 1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission: that is the safety net for everything else.

    Every other trophy is believed obtainable after the story, in free roam. Story missions are not replayable, but their trophies pop on completion, so none of them can be missed — only the balcony spot can.
